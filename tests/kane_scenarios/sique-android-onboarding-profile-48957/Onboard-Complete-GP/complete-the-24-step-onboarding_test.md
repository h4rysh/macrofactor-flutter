# Complete the 24-step onboarding questionnaire for weight loss

## Step 1
Complete the 24-step onboarding questionnaire for weight loss

A new user successfully completes the entire onboarding flow with a 'weight loss' goal and 'balanced' diet, transitioning to the dashboard upon completion.

Steps:
1. Launch the Sique mobile app for the first time.
2. Proceed through the initial welcome screens to begin the questionnaire.
3. Select the **Weight Loss** goal.
4. Select the **Balanced** diet type.
5. Select a protein preference.
6. Select a workout schedule (e.g., '3 days a week').
7. Enter valid body metrics for height, weight, age, and sex.
8. Review the projected target weight date and continue.
9. On the final step (Step 24), review the generated macro targets.
10. Tap the **Complete** or **Finish** button.
11. Verify the app transitions to the main dashboard.
