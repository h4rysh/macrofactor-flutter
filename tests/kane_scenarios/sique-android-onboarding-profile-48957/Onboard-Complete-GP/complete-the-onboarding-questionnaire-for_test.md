# Complete the onboarding questionnaire for weight gain with a keto diet

## Step 1
Launch the Sique mobile app for the first time.

## Step 2
Proceed through the welcome screens to start the questionnaire.

## Step 3
Select the **Weight Gain** goal.

## Step 4
Select the **Keto** diet type.

## Step 5
Complete the subsequent steps for protein preference and workout schedule.

## Step 6
Enter valid body metrics for height, weight, age, and sex.

## Step 7
Review the projected target weight date and continue.

## Step 8
On Step 24, review the generated macro targets.

## Step 9
Tap the **Complete** or **Finish** button.

## Step 10
