# Verify keyboard does not obstruct controls during body metrics entry

## Step 1
Launch the Sique mobile app.
Navigate through the questionnaire to a step requiring numeric input, such as 'What is your current weight?', and tap on the **Weight** input field.
Verify that the **Next** or **Continue** button is visible above the keyboard.
Enter a valid number in the **Weight** input field and tap the **Next** or **Continue** button.
