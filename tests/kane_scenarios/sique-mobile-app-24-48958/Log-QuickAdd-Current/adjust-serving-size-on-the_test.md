# Adjust serving size on the plate serving sheet before logging

## Step 1
Search for 'Oatmeal' and select it from the results. Change the quantity to '50' and the unit to 'g' (grams) on the serving size/unit field. Tap the 'Log' button. Verify the entry in the '09:00' slot.
