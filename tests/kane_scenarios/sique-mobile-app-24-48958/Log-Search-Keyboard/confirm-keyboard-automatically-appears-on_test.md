# Confirm keyboard automatically appears on the plate serving sheet

## Step 1
Confirm keyboard automatically appears on the plate serving sheet

This test verifies that after selecting a food item from search results, the keyboard is automatically presented on the plate serving sheet to facilitate quick portion entry.

Search for "Apple" and select the "Apple" result from the list.
Observe the screen immediately after the sheet appears.
Verify that the quantity or portion size input field is in focus.
