# Prevent setting a calorie target below the 1200 kcal safety floor

## Step 1
Navigate to the main **Strategy** screen. In the wizard, adjust the target rate of weight loss to an extreme value (e.g., 'Lose 2.0 lbs/week'). Observe the 'Daily Calorie Target' field and surrounding UI. Attempt to tap the **Confirm** or **Save** button. Navigate back to the **Strategy** screen without saving.
